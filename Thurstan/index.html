Our Legacy Milestones

A journey through time, honoring the heritage of Thurstan College.

1950

Government Senior School Opens

Opened as Government Senior School with 112 students and Principal Earle Schokman.

1953

Thurstan College Named

Officially renamed Thurstan College and adopted the motto "Lead us from darkness to light."

1957

Rugby Practices Commence

Rugby practices began, eventually leading to a dominant national presence.

1958

Science Block Opens

The first major Science block opened, and the school celebrated its first students entering university.

1968

Big Match Status

The Thurstan-Isipathana cricket encounter was elevated to Big Match status.

1970

National Cricket Honors

Amitha de Costa became the first Thurstanite to receive national cricket honors.

1978

Primary Section Reintroduced

The Primary section was reintroduced, strengthening the multi-generational alumni bond.

1992

National School Status

Officially elevated to National School status by the Education Department.

2000

Golden Jubilee

Celebrated its Golden Jubilee with the opening of a modern 25-meter swimming pool.

2022

Canada OBU Branch Formation

The Canada OBU branch was formed to connect and serve Thurstanites across Canada.
