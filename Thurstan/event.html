Annual Grand Reunion Dinner 2024

About the evening

The Annual Grand Reunion Dinner brings together alumni from every generation for a night of conversation, recognition, and renewed commitment to Thurstan College.

The program includes a welcome reception, dinner service, alumni awards, chapter updates, and fundraising commitments for student support initiatives.

Program

  • 6:30 PM: Registration and welcome reception
  • 7:30 PM: Opening address and dinner service
  • 8:45 PM: Alumni awards and scholarship update
  • 10:00 PM: Fellowship and entertainment
Oct 24, 2024

Event Details

  • Venue: Shangri-La Colombo
  • Time: 07:00 PM onwards
  • Audience: All alumni batches and invited guests
  • Dress: Formal / national dress
