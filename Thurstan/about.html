Our Legacy, Our Community

The Thurstan College Old Boys' Union stands as a testament to lifelong brotherhood and the enduring spirit of our alma mater.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

To foster a vibrant, globally connected community of alumni that actively contributes to the sustained growth and excellence of Thurstan College.

We empower members through networking, professional development, school infrastructure, scholarships, and meaningful service.
